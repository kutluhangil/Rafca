Carlos Ruiz Zafón

In Barcelona, 1945, young Daniel is taken by his antiquarian book dealer father to the Cemetery of Forgotten Books, a massive sanctuary where books are guarded from oblivion. He selects one book, The Shadow of the Wind, and sets out to find the author's other works, only to discover that someone has been systematically destroying every copy of every book the author has ever written.
