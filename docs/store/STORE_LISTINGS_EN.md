## 2. Full Description (Max 4000 characters)

Tired of scanning barcodes one by one or typing book titles manually?

Rafca is the next-generation bookshelf digitization companion for readers, collectors, and bibliophiles. Snap a single photo of your physical bookshelf; our advanced spine clustering and optical character recognition (OCR) algorithms segment each spine, match titles against open library catalogs, and build your digital archive in seconds.

### KEY FEATURES

🏛 **Nocturnal Archivist Design:**
Immerse yourself in an aesthetic inspired by historic midnight libraries: warm brass accents, deep obsidian dark surfaces, and editorial typography.

⚡ **Scan Entire Shelves at Once:**
Point your camera at a shelf; built-in roll and pitch level indicators guide your alignment. Interactive four-corner perspective warp and multi-angle OCR recognize up to 20+ books in a single capture.

🔍 **"Proof of Capture":**
Each book in your library preserves its original spine crop cut directly from your shelf photograph, complete with its dominant spine color. Your physical shelf remains connected to your digital catalog.

🛡 **Privacy First & Local-Only Images:**
Your photos and spine crops remain strictly on your physical device. No images are ever uploaded to remote servers or third-party clouds. Only plain text is sent ephemerally for catalog lookup. No ads, no tracking.

📚 **Shelves & Reading Status:**
Organize your volumes across custom shelves. Track reading statuses: Reading, Read, To Read, and Abandoned.

🎨 **Shareable Collection Cards:**
Turn your collection or individual shelf into an artistic archival card featuring typography, spine strip colors, and watermark. Share directly to Instagram Stories or messaging apps with one tap.

💾 **Open Data Ownership:**
Export your entire collection anytime in standardized RFC 4180 CSV or structured JSON. Your data is always yours.
